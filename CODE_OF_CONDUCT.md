# Code of conduct

D3B is made by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience: players who have never opened a
pull request, developers who have never played Dofus, and everyone between. This code says how we treat each other in
the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and the Discord server), so that all of them can take part.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Assume good faith, and remember that the person on the other side may be new to open
  source, to Rust or to English.
- **Discuss the work, not the person.** Disagree with reasons and, where you can, with evidence: a test, a measure, a
  capture described by its structure. Say what is wrong and why, not who is wrong.
- **Accept a no with its reasons.** Decisions are recorded with their reasons in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/README.md). Any
  of them can be revisited with new evidence; the same request repeated without it cannot.
- **Help newcomers find their way.** Point to the right document rather than telling someone to read the docs.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information (an account, an address, a capture of their game) without their consent.
- Asking for, or offering, help with what D3B never does: evading detection, mass accounts, payment automation,
  bypassing Ankama Shield.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, and encouraging any of the above.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer privately: a direct message on the
[Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/6A6Vw9Mn24), or a private report through the repository's
[Security](https://github.com/AerafalDev/D3B/security) tab. Reports are read in confidence, and the person reporting
is never exposed.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may, depending on the case, ask for a change of behaviour, remove or edit what was posted, or ban
someone from the project's spaces for a time or for good. The decision is explained to the person concerned.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
