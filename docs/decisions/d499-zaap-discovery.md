# Zaaps, the discovery of those the character does not know (D499)

A part of [the decisions](README.md); each decision has one line in [the index](../decisions-index.md). The zaaps known and
the teleports are [D83](d064-d090-step-4.md#d83-the-path-through-the-world-teleports-from-where-the-character-stands), the journeys [D74](d064-d090-step-4.md#d74-travelling-from-map-to-map), a zaap
taken only when it saves enough maps [D167](d126-d175-step-6.md#d167-a-zaap-only-when-it-saves-enough-maps).

## D499: An activity walks to every zaap within reach the character does not know, the nearest first, and ends once none is left

*2026-11-22, the owner's ask: "a route to discover every zaap of the game, those that are accessible and that the character
does not know yet". The owner chose the form and to leave the unactivated zaaps out, both as the lead recommended them.
Builds D74, D83, D87, D167, D484.*

- **What was found.** The server lists the zaaps the character knows again, whole, as it enters the map of one it did not
  know, and using the zaap teaches nothing more (client-game.md, "The game, teleported"): walking onto each zaap's map is
  the whole of the discovery. The pack's atlas holds the client's 62 zaaps with their maps (`WorldAtlas.Waypoints`), 15 of
  them not activated, none of which the client's world map draws (client.md). The world's pathfinder already says what is
  within reach: a sub-area closed to an account without a subscription is not entered, a transition is followed only when
  its criterion answers true, so one asking a quest, which the bot cannot answer, is not, and only a subscriber teleports.
- **What was made.** A module, `ZaapsModule` in `D3B.Bot.Zaaps`, registers the activity `zaaps`, "Discover the zaaps" /
  « Découvrir les zaaps », with no setting, so that it shows among the activities a character may be given with its defaults
  (D99), its card drawing the zaap glyph. `ZaapTargets` lists the activated zaaps the character does not know as it starts.
  Each step of `ZaapDiscoveryActivity` finds the nearest of those left by the world's path, travels there, and waits 5 s
  on its map for the server's list. The path is found again at each step, with the zaaps known so far, so a zaap just learnt
  shortens the next journeys, and one learnt on the way is not sought again. A teleport counts as 5 map changes, as the
  harvest's default (D167).
- **What it gives up.** A zaap no path leads to, whose journey fails or ends otherwise, or that the server does not list once
  the character stands on its map, is given up for the run: the activity never loops on it. The card counts the zaaps
  discovered of those sought, warns of each one out of reach by its sub-area, and draws the zaaps as a zone, to visit,
  visited or unreachable. A character busy at a step's start waits as the harvest's does (D484); one in its haven bag leaves
  it first.
- **Rejected.** A tour computed once, the shortest through every zaap: it would not use the zaaps learnt on the way, which
  change every distance after them, and the nearest-first search costs under a millisecond a step (D71). A route asset
  generated by the app: fixed when computed, for the same reason. A node of the behaviors' catalog as well, so that a profile's
  list of steps (D386) chains it: more work than the ask, left until someone wants it. Seeking the 15 unactivated zaaps:
  their meaning is not measured, and a walk to a zaap that may be gone costs more than it may teach.
- **Not measured.** The discovery run in game: whether the list always comes within 5 s of the map, the zaaps the server
  teaches on a map with two zones, and the fights met on the way, which the player plays unless the bot's switch has the AI
  play every fight.
